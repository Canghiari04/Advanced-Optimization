0. Useful notions
- V2G --> discharging vehicle batteries to the power grid.
- Formulated the global optimization problem, but impractical since it requires information from the future

1. Objectives
- Optimize scheduling for charging and discharging of eletric vehicles.
- Optimal EV charging patterns could reduce potential capital costs and minimize operational costs.

2. Decision variables
- M, charging and discharging performed during the day --> M^{CHG} + M^{v2G}, where the first one are the charging only, and the second one provide electricity to the grid.
- 

3. Principal constraints
- Find out the globally optimal scheduling solution which can minimize the overall charging cost.
- Difficult to find out the best scheduling solution for large EV population.
- Scheduling scheme should have the capacity to efficiently handle the random arrivals of the EVs.
- 

4. Assumptions
- Best way could be to charge EV battery when the demand is low and discharge the EV battery to the grid when the demand is high. 
- The losses between nodes are small.
- There is no congestion in trasmission.
- Working in a small geographical area.
- Neglect the spatial variation of the electricity prices.
- P^{max} all the vehicles have the same max charging power.
- We have historical data.
